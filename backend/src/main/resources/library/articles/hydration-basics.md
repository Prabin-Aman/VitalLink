There's no single number that suits everyone. Needs rise with heat, exercise and body size. Food provides some water too, especially fruit and vegetables.

## Simple signs you're doing well

- You rarely feel very thirsty.
- Your urine is pale yellow.

## Practical habits

- Keep a glass or bottle within reach and refill it at set times, such as with each meal.
- Drink before, during and after exercise, more so in the Texas summer heat.
- Log glasses in VitalLink's Nutrition view; the glass size is adjustable in your targets.

*This article is general information, not medical advice. People with heart or kidney conditions may be told to limit fluids; follow your clinician's guidance.*
