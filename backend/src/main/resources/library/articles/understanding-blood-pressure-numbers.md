A blood pressure reading has two numbers, in millimetres of mercury (mmHg):

- **Systolic** (top): the pressure when your heart beats.
- **Diastolic** (bottom): the pressure between beats.

## The categories used in the US

| Category | Systolic | | Diastolic |
|---|---|---|---|
| Normal | under 120 | and | under 80 |
| Elevated | 120–129 | and | under 80 |
| High, stage 1 | 130–139 | or | 80–89 |
| High, stage 2 | 140 or higher | or | 90 or higher |

VitalLink labels your readings with these categories. One high reading isn't a diagnosis; the pattern over days and weeks is what matters.

## Taking an accurate reading at home

1. Sit quietly for five minutes first, back supported, feet flat.
2. Rest your arm on a table with the cuff at heart level, on bare skin.
3. Avoid caffeine, exercise and smoking for 30 minutes before.
4. Take two readings a minute apart and keep both.

*This article is general information, not medical advice. A reading above 180/120, or any reading with chest pain, shortness of breath or weakness, needs emergency care: call 911.*
