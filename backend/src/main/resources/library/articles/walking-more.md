Adults should aim for at least **150 minutes of moderate activity a week**, such as brisk walking: about 30 minutes on five days. Short bouts count, so three 10-minute walks are as useful as one 30-minute walk.

## Easy ways to add steps

- Walk during phone calls.
- Get off one stop early, or park at the far end of the lot.
- Take a 10-minute walk after meals; it also helps steady blood sugar.
- Use the stairs for one or two floors.

## Setting a step goal

10,000 steps is a popular round number, not a medical threshold. A useful approach is to look at your current daily average in the Activity view and raise your goal by about 1,000 steps at a time.
