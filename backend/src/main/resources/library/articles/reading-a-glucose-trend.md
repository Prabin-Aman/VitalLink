A continuous glucose monitor (CGM) measures glucose in the fluid under the skin every few minutes. The value of a CGM is the **pattern** over a day and across days.

## What to look at

- **Time in range:** the share of the day your glucose stays within the range your clinician set.
- **After meals:** how high glucose rises and how quickly it comes back down.
- **Overnight:** whether levels stay steady or drift.

## Common patterns

- A steep rise after a particular meal can point to its carbohydrate content or portion size.
- A rise in the early morning can be a normal hormonal effect; mention it if it happens often.

CGM readings lag finger-stick readings by several minutes, especially when glucose is changing quickly.

*This article is general information, not medical advice. Use readings the way your care team has advised.*
