Resting heart rate is how many times your heart beats per minute when you are fully at rest. For most adults it sits between **60 and 100** beats per minute; regular endurance exercise often brings it lower.

## Why it moves

- **Fitness:** as aerobic fitness improves, the heart pumps more blood per beat, so it beats less often at rest.
- **Recovery:** a resting rate several beats above your usual can follow a hard training block, poor sleep, illness, alcohol or stress.
- **Medication and caffeine** can raise or lower it.

## Using the trend

Compare against your own baseline rather than other people. VitalLink's Heart tab shows your average over the last 90 days; a steady downward drift alongside regular exercise is a good sign.

*This article is general information, not medical advice. A resting rate regularly above 100, or below 50 with dizziness or fainting, is worth discussing with a clinician.*
