Adults benefit from muscle-strengthening activity on **two or more days a week**, working the major muscle groups: legs, hips, back, chest, shoulders, arms and core.

## A simple first plan

- **Squat** or leg press
- **Hip hinge** such as a Romanian deadlift or glute bridge
- **Push** such as a push-up or bench press
- **Pull** such as a row or lat pulldown
- **Carry or plank** for the core

Do 2–3 sets of 8–12 repetitions, finishing each set feeling you could have done two more.

## Progress gradually

Add a little weight or a few repetitions once every set feels comfortable, roughly once a week. Sharp or joint pain is a reason to stop and adjust; ordinary muscle soreness for a day or two is normal.

*This article is general information, not medical advice. If you have a heart condition, joint problems or are recovering from surgery, check with a clinician first.*
