For a **small, superficial burn**, such as a brief touch of a hot pan:

1. **Cool it** under cool (not ice-cold) running water for about 20 minutes.
2. **Remove** rings or tight items near the area before it swells.
3. **Cover** loosely with a clean, non-fluffy dressing or cling film.
4. **Relieve pain** with an over-the-counter pain reliever if you can normally take one.

## Don't

- Don't use ice, butter or ointments on a fresh burn.
- Don't pop blisters.

## Get medical help when

- The burn is larger than the palm of the hand, or deep, white or charred.
- It's on the face, hands, feet, genitals or a joint.
- It was caused by chemicals or electricity, or the person is a young child or an older adult.

*This article is general information, not medical advice. For serious burns, call 911.*
