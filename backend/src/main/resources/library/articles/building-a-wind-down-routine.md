A wind-down routine is a short, repeatable set of steps that signals the end of the day. Doing the same things in the same order helps your body expect sleep.

## A 30-minute starting point

- **30 minutes before bed:** set your phone to charge out of reach, or turn on a downtime or sleep focus mode.
- **20 minutes before:** dim the lights. Warm, low light is better than bright overhead light.
- **10 minutes before:** something calm and offline: a paper book, light stretching, or a few minutes of slow breathing.

## Make it stick

Start with one step and add others once it feels automatic. If you can't fall asleep within about 20 minutes, get up, do something quiet in low light, and go back to bed when you feel sleepy.
