A quick way to build a balanced meal is to divide your plate:

- **Half:** vegetables and fruit, in a range of colours.
- **A quarter:** protein such as fish, poultry, beans, tofu, eggs or lean meat.
- **A quarter:** whole grains such as brown rice, oats or whole-wheat bread.

Add a little healthy fat (olive oil, nuts, avocado) and drink water.

## Protein through the day

Spreading protein across meals, rather than saving most of it for dinner, helps with fullness and with building muscle if you strength train. VitalLink's Nutrition view shows protein, carbohydrate and fat against your targets.

## Keep it practical

Frozen vegetables and canned beans count. Logging meals for a couple of weeks is often enough to see patterns, such as a protein-light breakfast.

*This article is general information, not medical advice. If you manage diabetes, kidney disease or food allergies, a registered dietitian can tailor these guidelines.*
