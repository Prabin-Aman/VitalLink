Fainting is a short loss of consciousness, usually because blood flow to the brain drops briefly. Most people come round within a minute.

## How to help

1. **Lay them on their back** and, if you can, raise their legs about 12 inches.
2. **Check breathing.** If they are not breathing normally, call 911 and start CPR.
3. **Loosen** tight clothing at the neck.
4. When they come round, keep them lying down for a few minutes, then help them sit up slowly.

## Call 911 if

- They don't come round within a minute or two.
- They are injured from the fall, or have chest pain, an irregular heartbeat, trouble speaking or weakness on one side.
- They fainted during exercise, or are pregnant.
