Many people miss doses now and then, especially with medicines taken more than once a day. Taking them as prescribed is one of the most effective things you can do for a long-term condition.

## Routines that help

- **Anchor doses to daily habits**, such as brushing your teeth or breakfast.
- **Use reminders.** VitalLink can remind you on your phone and lets you mark each dose taken or skipped.
- **Keep a weekly pill organiser** if you take several medicines.
- **Plan refills early.** VitalLink shows when a refill is due within a week.

## If you miss a dose

The right action depends on the medicine. Check the leaflet or ask your pharmacist; for many medicines you take the dose when you remember unless it's nearly time for the next one. Don't double up unless you've been told to.

*This article is general information, not medical advice. Ask your pharmacist or prescriber about your specific medicines.*
